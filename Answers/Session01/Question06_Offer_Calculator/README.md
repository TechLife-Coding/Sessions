# Offer Calculator

A simple Python program that calculates the discount based on the purchase amount.

## Features

- Gets the purchase amount from the user.
- Applies a **15% discount** for purchases over **1,000,000**.
- Applies a **10% discount** for purchases between **500,000** and **1,000,000**.
- No discount for purchases below **500,000**.
- Displays both the discount amount and the final price.

## Discount Rules

| Purchase Amount | Discount |
|-----------------|----------|
| More than 1,000,000 | 15% |
| 500,000 to 1,000,000 | 10% |
| Less than 500,000 | No Discount |

## Example


buying price: 1200000

Offer: 180000.0
Final price: 1020000.0
